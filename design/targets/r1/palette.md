## Translation into target colours

M1 interprets the observed petrol/cyan and orange relationship with #123f49 shadows, #3c8d9d engraving, #b9c9c6 glints and #d87532 burnt-orange sunlight. These are design adaptations of the observed source, not literal sampled colours. M2 retains B’s violet family (#8053ae, #b184db) and adds restrained petrol/cyan orbit trails and tiny orange exhaust light. No yellow-gold is used with purple.

Both retain near-black #09090b, off-white #eeeae3 reading text and lime #c6f35a as the sole small UI accent. Orange, teal and violet remain artwork colours; UI links and rules stay neutral.
